The VerityRoad protocol

How civic evidence is reviewed

Three stages: submit, compare claims with the photograph, then strengthen the report.

Capture the scene, then state the claim

Use JPEG, PNG, or WEBP. Keep a safe vantage point. Write specific claims on separate lines so they can be assessed one by one.

Evidence taxonomy

What a photograph can and cannot do

Visible evidence

Supported by the image

A claim about a condition that is clearly shown, such as cracked asphalt next to a drain grate.

“Broken pavement around the grate is visible in the photo.”

Not measurable

Consistent, but incomplete

A hole may be visible while depth, width, or duration still need a scale object or extra angle.

“The cavity looks substantial, but 4-inch depth is not measurable here.”

External record

Unverifiable from the image

Ownership, dates, prior complaints, accidents, and legal responsibility need other records.

“The city ignored earlier tickets” cannot be shown by a photo.

          Uncertainty

            Methodology

            VerityRoad sends your photograph and claim text to a hosted Gemma 4 model. The server asks the model to list visible observations, grade each claim, name evidence gaps, and draft a cautious complaint. Results are checked against a JSON schema before they reach the browser.

            What the model is asked to do

            Describe only visible conditions. Compare each claim with those observations. Use statuses that distinguish support, incomplete measurement, absence from the frame, apparent conflict, external records, and insufficient information.

            Limitations

            This is assistive software, not an official inspection. Lighting, angle, occlusion, and model error can all produce incomplete or incorrect notes. Always review the output. A missing detail in a photo is not proof that a claim is false.

            Open the workspace

            Evidence standards

            supported_by_visible_evidence — the photo clearly shows the claimed visible condition.

            consistent_but_not_measurable — consistent with the photo, but size, depth, quantity, or duration cannot be established.

            not_visible_in_image — not shown; absence is not disproof.

            appears_inconsistent_with_image — visible conditions appear to conflict; still not a legal finding.

            requires_external_evidence — ownership, dates, accidents, prior tickets, or responsibility.

            insufficient_information — quality or context is too weak for a useful assessment.

            Photographs cannot prove exact pothole depth, road ownership, cause, accident history, legal responsibility, or exact location without independent records.

            Privacy and limitations

            There is no account system. Images and claim text are processed in server memory for a single analysis request and are not written to disk by this application. The hosted model provider receives the content needed to run the request under that provider’s terms.

            Do not upload images of bystanders, license plates, or other personal data unless you have a lawful reason. Avoid photographing from the travel lane.

            Generated drafts can be wrong. You are responsible for checking them before any official filing.
