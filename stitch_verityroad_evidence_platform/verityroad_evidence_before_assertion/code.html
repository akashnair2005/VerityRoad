EVIDENTIARY JURISPRUDENCE

Not everything written in a complaint can be proven by a photograph.

A credible civil petition honors the boundaries of what is visible. Conflating visual observations with assumptions weakens public oversight. Here is how our taxonomy separates distinct truth categories:

CATEGORY 01 Confirmed in image

Visual Observation

Features verifiable directly from pixel raster data, spatial contrast, and geometric orientation.

“Severe transverse fissure visible near storm drain casting with sub-base gravel exposed.”
Evidentiary Standard

Direct optical proof. Meets prima facie roadway defect requirements.

CATEGORY 02 Needs historical telemetry

Claim Needing Evidence

Plausible causal statements that single snapshot photos cannot substantiate on their own.

“Cavity causes recurring vehicle rim damage and rapid bicycle tire deflation during rush hour.”
Evidentiary Standard

Corroborated only when paired with accelerometer telemetrics or tire repair shop invoices.

CATEGORY 03 External administrative record

Unverifiable From Image

Administrative history or intent-based allegations outside the boundaries of optical recording.

“Department of Transportation ignored three prior resident tickets logged back in November.”
Evidentiary Standard

Routed to municipal CRM freedom-of-information loggers rather than image audit suites.
